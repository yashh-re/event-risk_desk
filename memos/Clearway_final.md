**The exposure**

Wind is your largest technology: 3,708 net MW, about 38% of capacity (FY2025 10-K). If wind output falls 5% below normal, we estimate $30.3M of revenue at risk, out of $1.4B in 2025 revenue. That comes from 10.5M wind MWh x ~$57.61/MWh (a price that blends wind, solar and storage) x a 5% shortfall. The cushion is thin: 2025 operating income was $160M against $387M of interest expense.

**How likely it is**

We estimate 20%. That is one low-wind year in five (2021-2025), so the sample is small. The 5% size is realistic and possibly conservative, since 2023 wind was roughly 4-5% below average and cost $43M.

**The hedge**

Event contracts pay $1 if the event happens. Price is $0.22 (20% probability plus a 2c spread).

Unhedged, the event costs -$30.3M.

**Recommended: Cover 50%.** It cuts the low-wind loss to -$18.5M while keeping the cost of a normal year to $3.3M, a middle path between the thin protection of 25% and the guaranteed $6.7M cost of 100%.

**What this doesn't cover**

- Your power contracts lock in price but not volume, which is exactly the gap this hedge fills. But the event contract pays on a measured wind-output trigger, not your exact output, so the payout may not match your real loss
- This covers low wind only. A different weather event, like the 2021 Texas freeze that cost $50M in settlements, wouldn't trigger it.
