## About

Hey! I'm Yashna, a Junior @ Indiana University studying Finance (Kelley School of Business) and Informatics (Luddy School), with a minor in Film Production. I like building tools where finance and technology meet.

Blindspot started with one question: what happens to a company when a single event goes wrong, like a snowstorm, a calm year for a wind farm or a soft opening weekend, and nothing in insurance or futures covers it? I picked 4 companies, two in sectors where event hedging is already being sold (aviation and AI compute) and two from my own interests (green energy and film), and tried to answer that question with real data.

### How I built it

- Read the 10-K filings for JetBlue, CoreWeave, Clearway Energy and Warner Bros. Discovery
- Wrote a Python script to analyze 10 winters of federal flight data (Bureau of Transportation Statistics) and measure JetBlue's storm shutdowns
- Built a set of superhero box office comps to estimate the odds of a soft opening for *Man of Tomorrow*
- Wrote a hedge pricer in Python, plus an AI agent (Claude API) that drafts each CFO memo using only my numbers and facts. I reviewed every draft and fixed what it got wrong, like a mislabeled revenue figure and a mix-up between power contracts and hedge contracts
- Used AI as a research and coding partner throughout. The judgment calls, like which companies to pick, which probabilities to use and what to exclude, are mine.
